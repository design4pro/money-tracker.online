Dark is where the pen palette is tightest: every wash clears the 7:1 floor, the tightest at 7.18:1, which is why the spec measures the composite rather than the token. The settled row is today's #523e7c, unchanged - and the one whose pooled trailing edge measures 5.98:1 against that floor.

The light washes here clear 7.5:1 even on the pooled trailing edge, so they stand whichever way Q8 is answered. The dark washes assume the decorative reading of that edge (1.4.11 at 3:1); if a glyph turns out to overlap it, dark drops to roughly a 15% hue mix and gets quieter still.
