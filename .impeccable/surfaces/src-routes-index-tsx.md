Scope: the public landing page `/`, and the first surface of the product's replacement visual
world (the whole product inherits it through `src/styles/tokens.css`). Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience: an adult at the kitchen table who already tracks money badly in a spreadsheet, often
in two currencies, sceptical because every other product wants a bank login. Action: open a free
workspace. Proof available: the product's own primitives rendered with static props. No social
proof exists and none may be invented (PRODUCT.md, Evidence on Hand).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A passbook, not a dashboard: every line is an entry and the balance stands after each
one. It refuses the centred hero over a dashboard screenshot and the grid of icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: Off-white form paper ruled in printed green; entries typed in Courier Prime; headings
and margin notes handwritten in Caveat, pinned to their row by a rule; one violet stamp ink for
every action and every "settled" mark; square corners, rules instead of cards, nothing floats at
rest; materials at full strength, never cream on cream. Dark: the same form on slate, bone ink,
lilac stamp. State is line form before hue: planned in pencil grey, booked in ink; nothing is
struck through, because the product has no voided line.

STORY: You keep this book yourself; the product types what you tell it, adds it up after every
line, and never asks a bank. So: open a free book.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Wordmark and Start free on the form's printed header rule. The refusal handwritten
across the page width at title scale on the first ruled lines. Directly beneath, the ledger typed
on the form: date, description, amount, and the balance-after column as the page's spine on the
right, the real LedgerPreview, with one handwritten margin note pinned to its first row.

FORM: "Książeczka" (the passbook), index 1 of seven, taken as IMPECCABLE'S PICK over dealt index 6.
Seed key 9d04b6c9. Code-led.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the
verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
